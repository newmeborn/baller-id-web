Everything around it is a mess.

If you play turf football with a group, you know this. The match takes an hour. The WhatsApp chasing takes all week. We built Baller.ID to fix three things.

WHAT BALLER.ID DOES

Add the turf cost. The app works out each share, writes the WhatsApp message for you, and keeps track of who has paid.

HOW IT WORKS

From the group chat to your card.

One match, start to finish. You keep using WhatsApp — Baller.ID just does the boring parts.

FAIR PLAY

Built so nobody can fake it.

A card is only worth something if it is true. These rules are why you can trust what you see on someone’s card.

✅

Stats count once your team agrees.

Your numbers stay pending until enough teammates add theirs. One person cannot decide the match alone.

🧾

An organiser cannot make up a result.

Every player marks themselves as playing and adds their own stats. A score with nobody behind it does not count.

🚩

Fake numbers get flagged.

Anyone on your team can flag a stat, and the captain decides. Until it is settled, nobody gets the points.

🗳️

Votes need the team.

Votes count once enough of the team agrees — so two friends cannot boost each other.

🔒

Teams lock before kick-off.

No changes in the last 30 minutes. Whoever is on the list is who played.

🗓️

Four matches a week per organiser.

Nobody can create fake matches just to collect points.

And no big promises. No trials, no “get scouted”, no paid anything. Just a true record of the football you actually play.

FAQ

Everything you want to know.

Is Baller.ID free?▾
Yes. Your card, your matches and the turf money split are all free. You will see a few ads in the app — that is how we keep it free. There are never ads on the money screens.
Does money go through Baller.ID?▾
No. You pay each other the way you already do, by UPI or cash. Baller.ID only keeps the list of who owes what and who has paid. We never take a cut.
Do all my friends need the app?▾
For the money, no — add friends who are not on the app by name, and they are part of the split. For stats and cards, yes. A match only counts when people who played back it up, and they need the app to do that. One person sets up the match; everyone else joins with a tap.
We played but nobody created the match. Can I still add it?▾
Yes. Use “Log a match” after the game. Add the score, who played and your stats. The people you add get asked if they played, and the match counts once one of them says yes.
How do you stop people faking numbers?▾
Your stats only count once your team agrees. Anyone who played can flag a number that looks wrong, and until it is settled, nobody gets the points. Votes also need enough of the team, so two friends cannot boost each other.
Is it only for 5-a-side?▾
No. Baller.ID works for turf 5s, 6s, 7s, 8s and bigger — the pitch view lays out whatever format you play.
What do ATK, DEF and REP mean?▾
Attack comes from goals and assists. Defence comes from saves and clean sheets. Rep comes from showing up and playing it straight. Turn your card over in the app to see exactly what built your rating.
Do I need to be a great player?▾
No. Showing up every week and being someone people want to play with again matter more than goals. A reliable player who never scores can still climb.
Is my data private?▾
Your card is yours. Only positive recognition is ever public — we don't publish anything that puts you on blast. See our Privacy Policy.
