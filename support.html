Common questions

Is Baller.ID free?

Yes. Building your card, tracking your matches and climbing the ranks is free.

How do you stop people faking stats?

Your stats only count once your team agrees. Anyone who played can flag a number that looks off, and until it's sorted, nobody gets the points — so two friends can't boost each other.

Is it only for 5-a-side?

No. Baller.ID works for turf 5s, 6s, 7s, 8s and bigger.

I signed in with the wrong account.

On the setup screen, tap "Not you? Log out" to sign out and start again with the right email.

How do I add a photo to my card?

Play your first match to unlock it, then tap edit photo on your card. Use a clear, solo, head-and-shoulders shot — the background is removed automatically.

Delete your account

You can request account and data deletion any time from our Delete Account page. See how we handle your data in the Privacy Policy.
